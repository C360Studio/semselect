# Frozen source-pilot cases and provenance

**Frozen after independent pre-run review.** Start with the [experiment result](README.md). [freeze.json](freeze.json) records the reviewed hashes and protocol. No candidate-model output was consulted or generated during authoring or review.

The 24 cases in [cases.json](cases.json) use the unchanged teaching instructions and two labels. They introduce six source families outside the teaching service-config/query-tiers and expense families. Each family has two `allow` and two `defer` cases. These are manually authored questions over selected pinned evidence, not recorded search results, production queries, or an estimate of general accuracy.

Code handles H01 and H02: a caller names an exact Go default fact; its supplied function contains the value, while its declaration-only counterpart does not. All other questions ask for multiple prose details, procedures or exclusions. No keyword baseline, parser-derived permission, or invented structured value is used to manufacture a model advantage. The source-derived fact sidecar is explicit experimental input, not a claim that SemSource already emits this schema.

[source records](sources.json) map every passage to an exact inclusive line range, original-file hash and commit. Complete pinned files and their repository licenses are under [source](source/). Passage text is verbatim, including line breaks. `source_excerpt` marks intact text; origin notes separately identify missing-part questions and evidence ablations. No facts were replaced with fictional alternatives. Metadata means the selected excerpts apply to the pinned-documentation question; it does not certify current deployed behavior or grant actions. ADR-0007's superseded branch-deletion debate is excluded.

The first normal-order trial is the primary 24-case view. A second trial and composite reversal test repeatability; they are not extra independent cases. Keep all examples from a source family together in future splits. The six families share two codebases and some concepts; this is a small source pilot, not blind held-out research, training-data isolation, or a representative benchmark. Missing-part and ablation construction are deliberate and may be easier than natural retrieval failures. Some negative cases include intact relevant distractor sections so evidence length is not a universal label cue. Freeze questions, evidence, labels and prompts before observing outputs; any subsequent change requires a new pilot revision and preserved prior results.
